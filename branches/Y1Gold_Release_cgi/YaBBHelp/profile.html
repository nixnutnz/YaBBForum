YaBB - Profiles


You can edit your personal profile, using the menu at the top of the forum. The following is a list of some of the information the profile contains:
Real Name
Real or displayed name.
Example: John Doe

Email Address
This must be valid to register, and things may be sent to this email address by the forum.
Example: me@mydomain.com
Birthdate
Your birthdate, in MMDDYYYY form. The birthday is only used to ensure that you are old enough to register on the forum. It also allows YaBB to display special items on your birthday.
Example: 07 31 1981

Time Offset
This is a + or - number to add or subtract to the current time showing on the forum.
Example: Forum time shows 13:28, but your time is 14:28. Time Offset would be +1

Signature
This is a brief message or list of urls's that you may type in. YaBBC code is allowed in your signature. Signatures are shown at the bottom of each of your posts.
Example: | my site | my other site |
